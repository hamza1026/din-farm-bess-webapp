What-if BESS simulator

Set solar, load, multiple WAPDA outage windows and generator assumptions. The simulator runs at 15-minute operating points, carries SOC continuously through the full 24-hour cycle, and can start the displayed day at any hour.

BESS sign convention: negative kW = charging, positive kW = discharging. BESS capacity defaults to 645 kWh and max charge/discharge defaults to 330 kW. When WAPDA is available, the generator is always OFF. In daylight, WAPDA contributes a varying minimum 10–15 kW while solar supplies the balance; surplus solar charges BESS. At night, WAPDA directly supplies the load. During a WAPDA outage, solar supplies load first; surplus charges BESS, while a solar deficit is supplied by BESS. If BESS energy is insufficient to sustain the full 15-minute interval down to Min SOC, the generator starts in that same interval and supplies the remaining deficit. Multiple blackout and load windows are supported.
